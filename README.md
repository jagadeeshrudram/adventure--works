# adventure--works
adventure works data analysis project 
# overview
This project focuses on analyzing AdventureWorks sales data to identify sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and regional sales trends.

The project combines two Internet Sales datasets and multiple dimension tables to create a unified dataset for business analysis and reporting.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.

- Identify top-performing products and product categories.

- Understand customer purchasing behavior.

- Analyze sales by region and country.

- Track monthly and yearly sales trends.

- Identify business growth opportunities through data-driven insights.
  
  # Tools & Technologies

- MySQL – Data storage, SQL queries, joins and analysis

- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning and analysis

- Power BI – Interactive dashboards and visualizations

- Tableau – Data visualization and business reporting

## Key Insights
- Total sales were about $29.36M, with a profit of about $12.08M (roughly 41% profit margin).
- A total of 60,398 orders were placed, with an average sale value of about 486.
- 2013 was the strongest year with sales of about $16M, far ahead of 2011 and 2012. 2010 and 2014 had very low sales, likely because the data for those years is incomplete.
- Sales show an upward trend through the year in the Tableau and Excel month-wise charts.
- Quarter-wise sales are fairly balanced: Q4 is the highest (about $9.1M), followed by Q3 ($7.6M), Q2 ($7.1M) and Q1 ($5.5M).
- Australia, Southwest, Northwest, United Kingdom and Germany are the top 5 regions by sales.
- Mountain-200 (Black and Silver) and Road-150 Red models are the top-selling products.
- Top customers include Jordan Turner, Willie Xu and Nichole Nara.
